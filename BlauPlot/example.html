BlauPlot — Example

Signal visualization and analysis · Blau Robotics

How to use:
Click "Load Demo Data" to see a generated signal, or click "Choose CSV File" to load your own data file.

Your CSV should have a header row with column names. Example:
time,signal_A,signal_B,signal_C,signal_D
0.00,1.2,0.5,-0.3,0.8
0.01,1.3,0.6,-0.4,0.9

Tip: Click any data point on the plot to add a value label. Drag to zoom, double-click to reset.
